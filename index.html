Race Plan

One goal time, split across every run, station and transition. Build it on your phone, sync it to the watch, and race against it: the main screen shows how far ahead or behind you are at every moment, and every lap gives its verdict. Stop doing the maths mid-race.

  1. 1Build. Set a goal. The draft splits it like a real race - drag what you know, lock it.
  2. 2Sync. Open the workout on the watch. The plan applies itself, target on every leg.
  3. 3Race. Ahead or behind, live. Blue ring on budget, orange at 90%, red past it.
