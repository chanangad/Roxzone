Refund Policy

Policy in effect since May 2026

Every ROXZONE purchase is backed by a 24-hour money-back guarantee. If the app isn't for you, for any reason, email us within 24 hours of purchase and you'll receive a full refund. No questions asked.

How to request a refund

Email hello@roxzone.app within 24 hours of your purchase. Include the email address you used at checkout, and if you have it handy, the claim code shown in the app (it starts with RZ-). That's everything we need.

How the refund is paid

The refund goes back to the original payment method through the payment provider you used at checkout. Depending on your bank or card provider, it can take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement.

What happens to the license

Once a refund is processed, the lifetime license attached to your claim code is deactivated and the app returns to its locked state on your watch.

Always refunded, regardless of the window

Two situations are covered at any time, not just in the first 24 hours:

  • Duplicate payments. If you accidentally paid more than once for the same license, the extra payment is refunded in full. Your license stays active.
  • Paid but never unlocked. If your payment went through but the app could not be activated on your watch and support was unable to resolve it, you receive a full refund.

After the 24-hour window

Requests made more than 24 hours after purchase are not covered by the guarantee. If one of the app's advertised features is not working properly on your device, please get in touch anyway; we will always try to fix a genuine problem, and we handle these cases individually.

Every feature is documented in detail in the User Guide, and a full walkthrough video shows the app in action, so you can see exactly what the app does, and how, before you buy.

Something look wrong with a charge?

If anything about a payment looks off, a duplicate charge, a wrong amount, a payment that didn't unlock the app, email us at hello@roxzone.app. Payment issues are typically resolved within hours.
