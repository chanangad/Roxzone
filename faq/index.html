Pricing & Compatibility

Is it a subscription or a one-time purchase?

One-time purchase. Pay once and keep it forever. No recurring fees, no annual renewal.

One license unlocks one watch at a time. If you upgrade your Garmin, you can move the license to the new watch for free with Restore - it doesn't stay unlocked on both.

Which Garmin watches are supported?

ROXZONE runs on Garmin watches that support Connect IQ 3.2 or later, including the Forerunner, Fenix, Epix, Venu, and Vivoactive series. The full and up-to-date device list is on the Garmin Connect IQ page.

Which payment methods can I use?

In India, checkout runs through Razorpay: UPI, cards and netbanking. Everywhere else it is card or wallet through our international checkout.

In mainland China, WeChat Pay is supported and no foreign card is needed - choose WeChat on the checkout page. Open the store page in Safari or Chrome rather than WeChat's built-in browser.

My claim code has more digits than the guide shows. Is that right?

Yes. From version 4.7.0 claim codes are 12 digits, shown in groups of four like RZ-4839 2017 5264. Apps installed before that show a shorter 8-digit code, and both work exactly the same way.

In the checkout field you can type it with or without the spaces - they are ignored either way.

I paid but the app still shows as locked. What do I do?
  1. Make sure your watch is paired with the Garmin Connect app on your phone and your phone has internet.
  2. Leave the unlock screen open for about 15 seconds - the app re-checks by itself while it's showing.
  3. Still locked? Close and reopen the app. On versions before 4.5.3 this isn't automatic: scroll down on the locked screen and tap Refresh License.
  4. If nothing works, try switching your phone between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Some networks block the license server.

Still stuck? Contact us with your claim code and payment email and we'll sort it manually.

License & Restore

Do I need to restore my license after reinstalling the app?

Usually not. From version 4.7.0 your claim code is tied to the watch itself, so it comes back unchanged after an uninstall and reinstall and the app unlocks itself when your phone is nearby.

Restore is still there for the cases that need it: a brand-new watch, or the one-off change if your app predates 4.7.0. Open ROXZONE, check the code on the locked screen, and if it differs from the one you bought with:

  1. Go to the Store → Restore tab.
  2. Enter the code now showing on your watch and the email address used for your original purchase.
  3. Click Restore Previous License, then open ROXZONE with your phone nearby. It picks up the license by itself.
I got a new watch. Can I transfer my license?

Yes. Install ROXZONE on your new watch, open the app to get that watch's claim code, then follow the same Restore steps using your original purchase email. The license transfers immediately.

The transfer moves the license rather than copying it: one license unlocks one watch at a time, so the old watch returns to locked.

I forgot my purchase email. How do I find it?

Check your inbox for the original payment receipt. That email address is the one tied to your license. If you're still stuck, reach out directly and we'll look it up.

Sharing Your Results

How do I share my result after a workout?

At the end of a session the watch offers a share link. Open it on your phone and the whole workout is there: every run, station and Roxzone transition with its own time and heart rate, plus totals and average pace.

From that page, Share gives you ready-made images - transparent overlay stickers in six styles, 9:16 story cards, and a square post card - to send straight to Instagram or save to your camera roll. Here is a sample result page.

Is my workout data uploaded anywhere when I share it?

Your workout saves to Garmin Connect exactly as it always does - a normal activity with every lap, and on to Strava if you have the two linked. That side is unchanged.

The share link is separate and adds no upload of its own. The result is encoded inside the link itself, so there is no ROXZONE account, no server copy, and nothing for us to lose or delete. Anyone you send it to can open it, and it never expires. Keep the link private and the result stays private.

How do I put my times on my own race photo?

On the result page, tap Share and pick an Overlay style. It downloads as a transparent PNG carrying your times and nothing else.

Then open your race photo in Instagram's story editor, add the PNG as a sticker, and drag or scale it wherever it sits best. Six styles are available, from a single total time to a full station-by-station panel.

Modes & Configuration

What race categories are supported?

All official HYROX categories with the correct station weights pre-loaded: Men's Open, Women's Open, Men's Pro, Women's Pro, Men's Doubles, Women's Doubles, Men's Pro Doubles, Women's Pro Doubles, and Mixed Doubles.

In Custom Hyrox you can use any category as your base, or set fully custom weights per station.

What is the Roxzone, and should I track it?

The Roxzone is the transition buffer between a run and a station (or vice versa): the seconds it takes to exit the track, reach your equipment, and begin. In a real race, a sloppy transition can silently cost you 10-30 seconds per station.

In Full Hyrox mode you can track each direction independently (Run → Station and Station → Run). In Custom Hyrox there's a single toggle covering both. When active, each transition becomes its own lap in Garmin Connect, so you can see exactly where the time is going and whether it's improving.

What does "Run Between Stations" do in Custom Hyrox?

When on (the default), ROXZONE automatically places a run before each station, following the standard HYROX format. When turned off, you switch to free-placement mode and can insert RUN slots manually anywhere in the reorder screen, including multiple stations in a row or runs back-to-back.

Will my Custom Hyrox setup be remembered between sessions?

Yes, completely. Your stations, order, weights, distances, and run distance are all saved automatically and restored exactly as you left them next time. Running a Full or Partial workout in between never touches your custom configuration.

Does the Touchscreen toggle disable physical buttons too?

No. The Touchscreen toggle only disables touch input during the workout. Physical buttons continue to work fully. It's useful for stations like Sled Push or Rowing where your hands may graze the screen.

During the Workout

I can't see my live pace during a run. Where is it?

Live pace is on a second screen. During any run segment, press the Down button (or swipe left) to bring up the run data screen. It shows elapsed time, distance covered, and your live pace. Outdoors, that pace is colour-coded against your target; indoors it's shown clean, with the bezel arc carrying your pacing cue instead.

What happens if I press Lap by mistake?

After every Lap press a brief confirmation screen appears. Tap Undo immediately to roll back. You can also access Undo Last Lap from the pause menu at any time. It covers the most recent Lap press only.

What is the looping behaviour in Custom Hyrox?

When you complete a full pass through your selected stations, the set counter increments automatically and the sequence loops. The current set number is shown on screen so you always know which round you're on.

Garmin Connect & Data

What activity type does ROXZONE save as in Garmin Connect?

The workout is saved as a Run activity named HYROX, appearing as Outdoor Run when GPS Mode was on, or Indoor Run for GPS-off sessions. This is normal and intentional; it ensures full compatibility with Garmin Connect's pace, HR, and lap data views.

Will the activity appear in Garmin Connect and Strava?

Yes. The workout syncs to Garmin Connect automatically when your watch connects to your phone. Each run, station, and Roxzone transition is a separate named lap. If Strava is linked to Garmin Connect, the activity appears there too.

Can I see splits for each station separately?

Yes. Open the activity in Garmin Connect and scroll to the Laps section. Every segment (each run, station, and transition) is its own lap, labelled by name, so there's no guessing what each row represents.

Why is it a Run and not a Workout or Cardio activity?

This is deliberate, and it's the single most important technical decision in the app.

Garmin's firmware only populates wrist-based pace and cadence when the activity is recorded as a Run. Record the same session as Training or Cardio and those fields come back empty — verified on the Fenix 8, where speed and cadence are simply null under the Training sport type. So a HYROX recorded as "Cardio" would show you no indoor pace at all, which defeats the point of pacing your runs.

Indoor sessions specifically use the Indoor Running sub-sport rather than Treadmill. Both give the same pace signal, but Treadmill brings Garmin's aggressive auto-pause and auto-stop, which fires the moment you stop moving — i.e. during every single station. Indoor Running behaves itself.

The trade-off is that these land in your feed as runs. See the next answer for how to relabel them.

How do I make HYROX sessions show as Workouts in Strava?

Every ROXZONE activity is named HYROX, which gives Strava something reliable to match on.

In Strava, create an auto-rename rule that matches the activity name HYROX and sets the type to Workout. It's a one-time setup and applies to every future session automatically — you never touch it again.

Worth knowing before you do: changing the type to Workout in Strava hides the pace and distance views. If you want to keep pace analysis on your runs, many athletes leave the type as Run and simply rely on the HYROX name to identify them.

Does indoor pace work without GPS or a foot pod?

Yes. With GPS Mode off, the watch derives pace and distance from its own wrist-based motion sensing — the same mechanism Garmin uses for indoor runs. No foot pod needed.

Accuracy improves once your watch has learned your stride, which happens automatically over a few outdoor GPS runs. If your indoor distances read consistently long or short, run outdoors with GPS a couple of times and the calibration corrects itself.

For an outdoor HYROX or a race, turn GPS Mode on and distance comes from GPS instead.

What is the "Station" column in my Garmin Connect laps?

ROXZONE writes a custom FIT field called Station on every lap, carrying that segment's real name — Ski Erg, Sled Push, ROXZONE, Warm Up, and so on.

That means your laps table reads as the workout actually happened, instead of "Lap 1, Lap 2, Lap 3…" that you have to decode from memory. Third-party tools that read FIT developer fields can pick this up too.

Strava does not display custom FIT fields, so lap names appear in Garmin Connect but not in Strava.

Is heart rate recorded per station?

Yes. Because the session records as a normal Run, all standard Garmin metrics come through: continuous heart rate, HR zones, calories, and per-lap averages. Each station and each run therefore carries its own average HR in the laps table.

Your maximum HR during runs is tracked separately too, which is what the shared result page uses.

Troubleshooting

The app crashed or is showing an error. What do I do?

Close and reopen the app. Most one-off crashes clear on relaunch. If the crash is repeatable, reinstall ROXZONE from Connect IQ and use the Restore tab to recover your license instantly. If it's tied to a specific watch model or action, send us a message with your watch model and the steps to reproduce.

The app shows "Check Connection". Is something wrong?

This appears when the watch can't reach the license server. Usually a temporary network issue, not a license problem. Make sure your watch is paired and your phone has internet; the app retries by itself, or tap Refresh License to check immediately. If it persists, try switching your phone between Wi-Fi and mobile data, as some networks block the server.

If you're already licensed, the app stays fully unlocked. Once connectivity is restored, everything re-verifies automatically.

My Custom Hyrox setup wasn't saved. What happened?

Settings save when you exit the config screen via Continue or Back. If you close the app mid-setup without doing either, the latest changes may not have been committed. Always exit cleanly to guarantee your setup is saved.
