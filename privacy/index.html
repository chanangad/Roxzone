Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Roxzone is built and maintained by Chan Digital. We collect the minimum data needed to operate the app and nothing more.

What we collect

  • Claim code: a device-generated identifier used to manage your license.
  • Email address: collected at purchase or restore, used only to verify your license.
  • Payment data: processed entirely by PayPal, Razorpay, or Dodo Payments. We never see or store card details.

What we don't collect

No GPS data. No health or biometric data. No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or analytics. No data from Garmin Connect.

Workout sharing

When you tap Share on the summary screen, your split data is encoded directly into the link: nothing is uploaded or stored on our servers. The link renders entirely in the viewer's browser. We cannot see your results.

Third-party services

Roxzone uses Garmin Connect IQ (distribution), PayPal / Razorpay / Dodo Payments (payments), Cloudflare (infrastructure), and Google Fonts (web fonts). Each is subject to their own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. Email chanangadhere@gmail.com with your claim code and we'll respond within 7 days.
