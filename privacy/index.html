Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ROXZONE is built and maintained by Chan Digital. We collect the minimum data needed to operate the app and nothing more.

What we collect

  • Claim code: A device-generated identifier used to manage your license.
  • Email address: Collected at purchase or restore, used only to verify your license.
  • Payment data: Processed entirely by Razorpay or Dodo Payments. We never see or store card details.
  • Workout results - only if you opt in. If you turn on Online Results on your watch, each workout you finish is saved (splits, totals, station settings, and average heart rate per segment) against your claim code, so it is there to view and share with a short link. If you never opt in, nothing is ever stored.

What we don't collect

No GPS data or location. No per-second sensor streams. No device identifiers or advertising IDs. No data from Garmin Connect. Heart-rate data (per-segment averages only) is stored solely when you have explicitly opted in to Online Results and shared that result - never otherwise.

Workout sharing

Sharing works in one of two ways, and you choose:

  • Default (no opt-in): your split data is encoded directly into the link itself - nothing is uploaded or stored on our servers, and the page renders entirely in the viewer's browser. Your workouts stay on your watch, which keeps the most recent 20.
  • Online Results (opt-in): if you accept the one-time prompt on your watch (or turn on the Online Results toggle in workout settings), each workout you finish is uploaded automatically and gets a short link that keeps working, so your results are not lost when the watch drops the oldest of its 20. Only finished workouts are saved; nothing else on your watch is uploaded.

Shared pages show only the workout itself. They never display your name, email, or claim code, and cannot be used to browse anyone's other results.

Your consent and your data

Storing heart-rate data requires your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), which is why Online Results is strictly opt-in and off by default. Consent is asked once, on your watch, in plain words - and declining changes nothing about how the app works.

  • Withdraw anytime: turn Online Results off in the watch's workout settings. Uploading stops immediately; nothing new is saved. Results already stored stay until you ask us to delete them.
  • Delete stored results: email hello@roxzone.app with the name and email address you purchased with, and we will delete all stored workout results within 7 days. (Payment records are retained as required by tax law.)
  • Where: data is stored with Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

Your account and signing in

You can sign in at roxzone.app/me to see the results you have chosen to store. There is no separate signup and no password: your account is simply the email address you bought ROXZONE with.

  • How signing in works: you enter your email and we send a six-digit code. We store a one-time code, hashed, that is valid for 15 minutes and refused once used. We never store the code itself.
  • Staying signed in: a strictly necessary cookie keeps you signed in so you do not have to request a code every visit. It holds no personal data and no tracking identifier - only a random session reference, which is stored hashed on our side. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies anywhere on this site, which is why you are not asked to accept any.
  • What the account holds: your email address, the sign-in times of your current sessions, an optional display name, and any names you give your watches. Nothing else.
  • How long: sign-in codes are deleted as soon as they are used or expire. Sessions expire after 90 days of not being used and are deleted after that. Signing out ends the session immediately.
  • What you can do there: view and share your stored results, export everything we hold as a file, and delete all your stored results yourself, without emailing us.

Signing in only ever shows results from watches licensed to your own email address. It never displays anyone else's data, and a shared result link never reveals whose account it belongs to.

Third-party services

ROXZONE uses Garmin Connect IQ (distribution), Razorpay / Dodo Payments (payments), Cloudflare (infrastructure), Resend (sending sign-in and invoice emails), and Google Fonts (web fonts). Each is subject to their own privacy policy.

Your rights

The fastest route is to sign in, where you can export everything we hold and delete your stored results yourself, straight away. You can also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by email at any time: write to hello@roxzone.app from the address you purchased with, and we'll respond within 7 days.
