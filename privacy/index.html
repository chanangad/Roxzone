Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ROXZONE is built and maintained by Chan Digital. We collect the minimum data needed to operate the app and nothing more.

What we collect

  • Claim code: A device-generated identifier used to manage your license.
  • Email address: Collected at purchase or restore, used only to verify your license.
  • Payment data: Processed entirely by Razorpay or Dodo Payments. We never see or store card details.
  • Workout results - only if you opt in. If you turn on Online Results on your watch, then each time you share a result we store that workout (splits, totals, station settings, and average heart rate per segment) against your claim code so your link keeps working. If you never opt in, nothing is ever stored.

What we don't collect

No GPS data or location. No per-second sensor streams. No device identifiers or advertising IDs. No data from Garmin Connect. Heart-rate data (per-segment averages only) is stored solely when you have explicitly opted in to Online Results and shared that result - never otherwise.

Workout sharing

Sharing works in one of two ways, and you choose:

  • Default (no opt-in): your split data is encoded directly into the link itself - nothing is uploaded or stored on our servers, and the page renders entirely in the viewer's browser.
  • Online Results (opt-in): if you accept the one-time prompt on your watch (or turn on the Online Results toggle in workout settings), sharing uploads that result to our servers and gives you a short link that keeps working. Only results you actually share are stored - never workouts you didn't share.

Shared pages show only the workout itself. They never display your name, email, or claim code, and cannot be used to browse anyone's other results.

Your consent and your data

Storing heart-rate data requires your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)), which is why Online Results is strictly opt-in and off by default. Consent is asked once, on your watch, in plain words - and declining changes nothing about how the app works.

  • Withdraw anytime: turn Online Results off in the watch's workout settings. Storage stops immediately; no new results are uploaded.
  • Delete stored results: email hello@roxzone.app with the name and email address you purchased with, and we will delete all stored workout results within 7 days. (Payment records are retained as required by tax law.)
  • Where: data is stored with Cloudflare, our infrastructure provider, and is never sold or shared with anyone.

Third-party services

ROXZONE uses Garmin Connect IQ (distribution), Razorpay / Dodo Payments (payments), Cloudflare (infrastructure), and Google Fonts (web fonts). Each is subject to their own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. Email hello@roxzone.app with the name and email address you purchased with, and we'll respond within 7 days.
